03 — How I work now

AI is my force-multiplier. I'm the one steering.

My edge isn't that AI writes code. It's that 25 years of engineering judgement now ships far faster, because I architect, I decide, I review, and a directed swarm of agents does the rest. Managed like a team, never trusted like an oracle.

"Speed is a multiplier, not a creator. Multiply zero understanding by 100×, you still get zero." AI accelerates whatever you already bring. With sound architecture and real understanding underneath, it turns one engineer into a team. Without them, it just ships problems faster. That's why I keep the judgement human.

Less typing, far more thinking

I write a fraction of the code by hand, but review, direct and understand far more than I used to.
